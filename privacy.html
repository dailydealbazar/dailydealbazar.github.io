Privacy Policy

Your Privacy Matters

At Daily Deal Bazar, your privacy is important to us. This website is a simple deals-sharing site. You can browse our pages and explore deals without creating an account, signing in, or giving us your personal information.

We built this site to be light and simple: no accounts, no profiles, and no forms asking for your details just to look at a deal. You are free to visit, browse, and leave at any time, and we do not track who you are as an individual.

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, home address, payment details, or any other personal data. We do not ask you to register, log in, subscribe, or fill in a form to view deals. We do not sell products directly, so we never ask for order or delivery details. Any purchase you make happens on AliExpress, not on this website.

In short, there is nothing for you to sign up for here, and nothing we need from you to use this website. If you ever buy something, you give your details to AliExpress and the seller on their own website, under their own privacy rules — not to us.

Cookies

Your browser or third-party services linked from this site may use cookies. Cookies are small files that can remember preferences, measure how a site is used, and help services work properly. You can clear or block cookies in your browser settings at any time. The site will still work if you do, although some features of linked websites may behave differently.

We do not set advertising cookies of our own on this website. Any cookies you meet here are the basic ones that come with the hosting and linked services, used to run the site and to understand, in general terms, which pages visitors find useful so we can improve the site.

Third-Party Links

Our pages contain links to AliExpress and possibly other third-party websites. When you click one of those links, you leave Daily Deal Bazar and the privacy policy of that other website applies. We do not control those websites, their cookies, or how they collect and use information. Please read the privacy policy of AliExpress or any other site before you buy anything or share information there.

This applies to every link that takes you away from Daily Deal Bazar, including product links, seller pages, and any other external link we may add in future. Your relationship with those websites is between you and them.

Children's Privacy

This website is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent and believe a child has shared information with a linked third-party site, please contact that site directly.

Parents and guardians: because we do not collect personal information from anyone on this website, there is nothing for a child to share here. If a child does contact us by email, please let us know, and we will delete the message and will not use the information in it.

Your Rights

Because we do not collect personal data on this site, there is no personal data held by us for you to view, correct, or delete. You remain free to browse without an account, clear cookies in your browser, and choose whether or not to click any third-party link.

If you do contact us by email, you can ask us to delete your message at any time, and we will do so. If you have any question about your privacy, or if you want a message you sent us deleted, please email us using the contact email below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if we add a contact option. Any change will be shown on this page, so please check it occasionally.

Please check this page from time to time if you want to stay up to date. The version published here is the current version, and it applies from the date shown on this page.
