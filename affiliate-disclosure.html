Affiliate Disclosure

Quelia Finds is a product-discovery website. It is free to use, and it is supported by affiliate commissions. This page explains exactly how that works.

What is an affiliate link?

Most product links on this website are affiliate links to AliExpress. When you click a product link, you go to that product's page on AliExpress. If you buy the product there, AliExpress or the seller may pay us a small commission.

Does it cost you anything?

No. The commission is paid by AliExpress or the seller — it is not added to your bill. The price you pay is the price shown on AliExpress at the time you buy.

Does the commission change what we show you?

No. A product is never shown only because it pays a commission. We look for products that are useful, fairly priced, and likely to be enjoyed in daily life, and we share the ones that are truly worth a look. If a product stops being good value, we stop sharing it.

We do not sell products

Quelia Finds does not sell products, take orders, or handle payments, shipping, or delivery. Your order, payment, shipping, and delivery are handled by AliExpress and the seller. If you have a question about an order, please contact AliExpress or the seller directly.

Prices

Prices, discounts, stock, and offers can change at any time without notice. The final price, shipping cost, and delivery time are shown on AliExpress at the time you buy. A discount shown on this site is only a guide — if the price on AliExpress is different, the AliExpress price is the one that counts. When a product shows a struck-through price, that is the original price listed by the seller on AliExpress — we never invent original prices.
