Frequently Asked Questions

Honest answers about how Daily Deal Bazar works. If your question is not here, email us at atifhussain.

How do I buy a product?

Click "BUY NOW" on any product and it opens that product's page on AliExpress in a new tab. You place the order and pay on AliExpress. Daily Deal Bazar does not sell products directly and has no checkout of its own.

How do I pay?

Payment is made on AliExpress, using the payment methods AliExpress offers you at checkout. There is no Cash on Delivery through this website — we never collect payment from you.

How long will delivery take?

Delivery time varies by seller and by the country you ship to. Check the estimated delivery shown on the AliExpress product page before you buy — that is the only delivery estimate that counts.

What if my order arrives damaged, or does not arrive?

Orders are covered by AliExpress Buyer Protection. If something is wrong with your order, open a dispute or a return request on AliExpress — that is where your order lives.

Can I return a product?

Returns follow the AliExpress seller's policy and AliExpress Buyer Protection. Start the return on AliExpress. We cannot process returns here because we never hold your order or your payment.

Are prices in USD or in my currency?

Prices are listed in US dollars. The country selector at the top of the page converts them to your local currency using current exchange rates, so you can compare easily — and on product pages you will also see the approximate price in Pakistani Rupees. The price you finally pay is the one shown on AliExpress at checkout.

The price on AliExpress is different from the price here — why?

Prices and discounts on AliExpress change often. The price on the AliExpress product page at the moment you buy is the final price.

Do you deliver to my country?

We do not ship anything ourselves — every order is shipped by the AliExpress seller. Check the "Shipping to" section on the AliExpress product page to see whether the seller delivers to your country.

Is the discount real?

When a product shows a struck-through price and a % OFF badge, that is the original price listed by the seller on AliExpress compared with the current sale price. We never invent original prices.

How do I contact you?

Email us at atifhussain — for example if a deal no longer works or a price has changed. We will do our best to reply as soon as we can.
